# Surface brief: home (index.html)

Scope: single-page portfolio. Mode: Experience. Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career drawn as a metro network map. Each job is a line, each shipped result a station, and skills carried between jobs are interchanges. Refuses the dark dev portfolio: neon-on-black, 3D blob, stat cards, tabbed timeline, numbered sections.

OWN-WORLD: Transit-map paper ground (cool off-white; deep night-sheet in dark mode), saturated line colours as the palette (Red, Yellow, Blue for jobs; Green, Violet, Magenta for branch lines), white stations with ink rings, larger interchange rings, line badges as the only "chips". Signage type (Overpass, the free Highway Gothic), one family. Deep signage-navy panel for the terminus (contact).

STORY: The visitor sees in one glance three lines across four years, each station a number they can trust; follows a station into its strip map for detail; reaches the terminus and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width 16:9 map. Title block in the top-left corner (name, role, one sentence, Email + Resume + GitHub + LinkedIn). Lines run bottom-left to top-right; Blue line ends at a "You are here" terminus. Legend band and Pause trains control under the map. Below 1024px: title block, then a vertical route overview.

FORM: Code-led. Signature interaction: the flat printed map lifts into a tilted 3D network (Three.js) and trains start running along each line; station labels are links into the strip maps; hovering a line or legend entry isolates it. Reduced motion stays on the flat map. Seed: none (unavailable; impeccable engine blocked in this environment).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
